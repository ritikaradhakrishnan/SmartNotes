HELLO, SMARTNOTES

A notebook you can ask questions.

This public edition saves notes in your browser. It has no AI chat or sign-in, and it does not sync with the connected app. Export a backup before clearing browser data.

How the connected app works

The local Next.js app uses Clerk for sign-in and MongoDB with Prisma to save your notes. When you ask a question, it finds relevant notes and gives their text to a model. Think of an open-book question: find the right page first, then write the answer.

What is RAG?

Retrieval-augmented generation means retrieving information, adding it to a question, and generating an answer using that context. It does not train the model on your notes. The local edition matches keywords across your 200 most recently edited notes and supplies up to five shortened excerpts, with numbered references you can check.

The original OpenAI/Pinecone approach used embeddings to search by meaning. Keyword retrieval is simpler, but can miss synonyms and details outside the selected notes. References are useful pointers, not a guarantee of accuracy.

The model and its tradeoffs

The default connected setup uses Qwen3 4B through Ollama. Qwen is the model; Ollama runs it on your computer. “4B” refers to roughly four billion learned parameters. Your notes provide context rather than special training.

Local inference needs no paid API balance and keeps model requests on your computer. It also uses your memory and processing power, so speed depends on hardware. Larger models may handle more complicated questions better but usually need more resources. Hosted models shift that burden to a provider, with cost, network, and data-handling tradeoffs. This project has not benchmarked those alternatives.

Clerk and MongoDB still need internet. The connected app returns completed answers rather than streaming them. Download and configure the local edition to try it; this browser demo does not connect to your model.
